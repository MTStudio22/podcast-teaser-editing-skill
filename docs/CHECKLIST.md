# Podcast Teaser Editing — Checklist

Run through this before and during every podcast teaser cut. Full reasoning and proof: [`RULES-AND-MATH.md`](./RULES-AND-MATH.md).

## A. Pre-production requirements (before opening the timeline)

- [ ] Total runtime of the raw podcast is known (sets your target trailer-length band — see C)
- [ ] Avatar is profiled in 5 questions before touching a single clip: who are they, what do they do, their income/budget level, what they're here to learn/get, what stage of that journey they're at
- [ ] Speaker's baseline energy level is assessed on a quick watch-through (high vs. needs-enhancing) — sets your music BPM lane
- [ ] A full, uncut watch of the raw podcast has happened at least once, at normal speed, no scrubbing
- [ ] You know which of the 3 ending-bridge types (status-upgrade/ego-hit, perceived value, authority-with-proof) fits this avatar, decided before you go looking for the closing line
- [ ] You're clear this is a single-avatar cut — no clip gets in because the editor personally finds it interesting

## B. The editing protocol (in order)

- [ ] Build timeline, drop the full raw podcast on it
- [ ] Watch it once more inside your editor, calmly, start to finish
- [ ] Mark every candidate moment as you go, color-coded: green = certain fit, orange = backup/maybe, red = questions (tracked against the 30% cap)
- [ ] For each marked clip, check whether it can serve 2+ markers at once — those are your highest-priority picks
- [ ] Duplicate the timeline ("stacked timelines") — original marked timeline stays untouched as reference
- [ ] On the new timeline, pull marked clips up and arrange for pacing
- [ ] Place pattern interrupt(s): 1 interrupt → 45–55% of trailer length; 2 interrupts → ~30% and ~80%
- [ ] Lay in music at the BPM lane decided in A (100 BPM if energy's already high, 150–190 to enhance); switch tracks where energy/context shifts, ideally syncing a switch to a pattern interrupt
- [ ] Build the ending bridge in the final ~15 seconds using the single bridge type chosen in A
- [ ] Confirm the final 15 seconds got as much attention as the first 15 (opening hook)

## C. Final quality gate (before export)

| Check | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Trailer length vs. podcast runtime | 1hr–1h45m source → 1:10–1:30 trailer. 2hr+ source → 50s–1:10 trailer |
| Total statement count | <1 min trailer: 7–9 bold statements. 1–1:40 trailer: 10–13 max |
| Question share | ≤30% of total statement weight |
| Pattern interrupts | 1–2 present, correctly placed |
| Ending bridge | Exactly one of the 3 types present in the final ~15s, matched to the avatar |
| Every clip | Passes "context or clutter" — cut for avatar, not ego |
| Music | Matches the BPM lane for speaker energy; no unmotivated switch |
